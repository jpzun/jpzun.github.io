I am a Math Fellow (formerly Assistant Adjunct Professor) at the [UCLA Department of Mathematics](https://ww3.math.ucla.edu/), working with [Joaquín Moraga](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~jmoraga/). I received my Ph.D. from [Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile](https://www.uc.cl/en) under the supervision of [Giancarlo Urzúa](https://www.mat.uc.cl/~urzua/). Previously, I was a master's student at PUC and an undergraduate student at [Universidad de los Andes](https://www.uniandes.edu.co/es) under the supervision of [Cristian Martínez](https://sites.google.com/site/cristianmathinez/home).

My research interests lie in algebraic geometry. I am particularly interested in:

- Deformations and degenerations of algebraic varieties;
- Birational-geometric methods in the study of compactifications of moduli spaces;
- Cox rings of non-Mori dream spaces.
